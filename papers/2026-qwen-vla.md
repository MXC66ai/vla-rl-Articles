## Key Idea
Extends Qwen's vision-language stack into a unified VLA model for heterogeneous embodied decision-making — manipulation, navigation, and locomotion in one architecture.

## Architecture
- **Backbone**: Qwen VL stack
- **Training**: Unified pre-training across embodiments and tasks
- **Key Innovation**: Single model handles multiple robot morphologies

## Our Take
Demonstrates that VLA unification across embodiments is feasible at scale — a step toward general-purpose embodied intelligence.
