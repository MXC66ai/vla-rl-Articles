## Key Idea
First open-source VLA model handling 36-DoF bimanual dexterous manipulation, with interaction-graph-based contact reasoning and implicit MPC.

## Architecture
- **Key Innovation**: Interaction graph for dual-arm coordination + implicit MPC
- **Action**: High-DoF continuous control
